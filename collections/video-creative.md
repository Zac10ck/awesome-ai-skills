# Video and creative coding

Tools for programmatic video, editing, generation, animation, and 3D workflows.

| Workflow | Resources |
|---|---|
| React-based video | [Remotion](https://github.com/remotion-dev/remotion) and [Remotion Agent Skills](https://github.com/remotion-dev/skills) |
| Scripted editing | [MoviePy](https://github.com/Zulko/moviepy) and [FFmpeg](https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg) |
| Desktop editing | [Kdenlive](https://invent.kde.org/multimedia/kdenlive) and [Shotcut](https://github.com/mltframework/shotcut) |
| 3D and animation | [Blender](https://github.com/blender/blender) and [Blender MCP](https://github.com/ahujasid/blender-mcp) |
| Generative video | [Wan Video](https://github.com/Wan-Video/Wan2.1) and [Diffusers](https://github.com/huggingface/diffusers) |
| Motion for interfaces | [GSAP Skills](https://github.com/greensock/gsap-skills) and [motion-framer](https://github.com/freshtechbro/claudedesignskills/tree/main/.claude/skills/motion-framer) |

Check model licenses, likeness rights, training-data policies, and platform rules before publishing generated media.
