# Programming foundations

A compact map of the language, backend, data, and automation foundations represented in this directory.

## Languages and runtimes

- [Python](https://docs.python.org/3/) — general programming, automation, data, and AI.
- [TypeScript](https://github.com/microsoft/TypeScript) and [Node.js](https://github.com/nodejs/node) — web, backend, and tooling.
- [Rust](https://github.com/rust-lang/rust) — performance-sensitive and systems software.
- [Go](https://github.com/golang/go) — cloud services and infrastructure tooling.
- [Kotlin](https://github.com/JetBrains/kotlin), [Swift](https://github.com/swiftlang/swift), and [.NET](https://github.com/dotnet/runtime) — mobile, desktop, server, and cross-platform development.

## Backend and APIs

- [FastAPI](https://github.com/fastapi/fastapi), [Django](https://github.com/django/django), [NestJS](https://github.com/nestjs/nest), and [Spring Boot](https://github.com/spring-projects/spring-boot).
- [Appwrite](https://github.com/appwrite/appwrite), [PocketBase](https://github.com/pocketbase/pocketbase), and [Directus](https://github.com/directus/directus) for ready-made platform capabilities.

## Data foundations

- Relational: [PostgreSQL](https://www.postgresql.org/), [SQLite](https://www.sqlite.org/), and [DuckDB](https://github.com/duckdb/duckdb).
- Search and cache: [Elasticsearch](https://github.com/elastic/elasticsearch) and [Redis](https://github.com/redis/redis).
- Vector retrieval: [Qdrant](https://github.com/qdrant/qdrant), [Milvus](https://github.com/milvus-io/milvus), [Weaviate](https://github.com/weaviate/weaviate), and [Chroma](https://github.com/chroma-core/chroma).
