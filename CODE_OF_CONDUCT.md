# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive, and evidence-driven. Welcome newcomers, disclose conflicts of interest, respect project licenses, and focus criticism on ideas and contributions rather than people.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, spam, deceptive promotion, and deliberate distribution of malicious resources are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method on their GitHub profile. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This compact policy is inspired by the principles of the Contributor Covenant.
